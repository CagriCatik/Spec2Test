A reminder about what these numbers can and cannot say:
  Fewer open questions is not a win on its own. A model that stops
  asking because context made it confident is worse, not better. Read
  'questions' together with 'unsupported values' and with the review
  list; the drafts themselves still need a tester's judgement.

  'context values actually used' counts only C_VALUE_FROM_CONTEXT: a
  number lifted from a retrieved passage. A passage that explained an
  operating state, a signal mapping or a fault reaction without
  contributing a figure scores zero here and may still have helped.
  Declining to take a value whose applicability is unclear is correct
  behaviour, not a failure. The qualitative pair review answers what
  this column cannot.

  The 20 requirements were chosen because a prose question exists for
  them, and those same questions were used to evaluate the retrievers.
  This is a diagnostic comparison on known tasks, not an independent
  final test, and not representative of the whole corpus.
